About MATEX Dallas

A nonprofit organization serving the Dallas Metroplex.

Our Story

Rooted in Community. United by Heritage.

2006 Founded

As the Mandingo population in the Dallas–Fort Worth area continued to grow, community members recognized the need for an organization that could bring families together, preserve our cultural heritage, and strengthen connections within the community.

In 2006, members of the community came together and formally organized the Mandingo Association of Texas – Dallas (MATEX Dallas, Inc.).

Since its founding, MATEX Dallas has served as a place for Mandingo families and community members to connect, celebrate their culture, support one another, and work together to build a stronger community for future generations.

MATEX Dallas, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization.

Community Impact

Responding When Help Was Needed

During the Ebola crisis in West Africa, MATEX Dallas organized a fundraising effort to support organizations working to combat the outbreak and assist affected communities.

The initiative reflected MATEX Dallas’s commitment to humanitarian service and to supporting communities during times of crisis.

Announcement of MATEX Dallas’s Ebola relief fundraising effort.

Our Mission

What We Stand For

MATEX Dallas is dedicated to uniting and empowering the Mandingo community in the Dallas–Fort Worth area by preserving our cultural heritage, supporting education and youth development, strengthening families, and promoting community service. We strive to create opportunities, provide support to those in need, and build a strong, inclusive community where current and future generations can thrive.
