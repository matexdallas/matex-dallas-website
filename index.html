Our Mission

Why We're Here

MATEX Dallas is dedicated to preserving Mandingo culture, strengthening family values, promoting education, supporting community development, and fostering unity among members while serving the broader Dallas–Fort Worth community with integrity and compassion.

Who We Are

One Community. One Family.

MATEX Dallas brings members, families, and friends together to strengthen community relationships, preserve cultural heritage, support education, and encourage service. Our activities create opportunities for connection, celebration, leadership, and community involvement.
