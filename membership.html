Become a Member

Join MATEX Dallas and become part of One Family. Membership helps support our programs, community activities, and our long-term goal of acquiring a permanent MATEX Community Center.

Members are encouraged to pay their annual membership dues in full whenever possible. Paying in full provides MATEX Dallas with stronger financial resources to support our programs and work toward acquiring a permanent community center. Members who are unable to pay the full amount at once may make partial payments throughout the year.

Why Pay in Full?

When members pay their annual dues in full, MATEX Dallas has greater financial stability to plan programs, serve our community, and work toward our goal of acquiring a permanent MATEX Community Center. We understand that every family’s financial situation is different, so partial payments are always welcome.

Every payment helps us move closer to a home for our community.

How to Join

Reach out

Apply online or contact MATEX Dallas to express interest in becoming a member.

Choose your membership

Select Single or Couple Membership based on your household.

Pay your dues

Pay your annual registration and dues in full, or arrange partial payments throughout the year.

Welcome to the family

Once registered, you’re officially part of One Family and can take part in MATEX Dallas programs and activities.
